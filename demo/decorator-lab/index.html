💡 Decorator Design Principle

          
Decorators are pure or stateful pipeline transformers. By piping them inside the Adapter, the raw data is normalized, smoothed, and snapped before reaching the audio rate synthesizer.

          
The visual UI and the .mappings.json document are strictly bidirectional with zero GUI-only state.
